## Short version (footer link text: "Privacy notice")

This dashboard is used by barangay staff to dispatch Tanods, record
incidents and keep barangay records. It holds personal information, so
access is limited by role and records are kept for set periods.

## What this dashboard holds

- **Incident reports**: the type, place, time and status of an incident,
  and the names and contact numbers of complainants and respondents where
  recorded.
- **Confidential narratives**: the written account of an incident. Only the
  Barangay Secretary can read it.
- **Tanod positions**: the location of Tanods on duty, while they are on duty,
  so they can be dispatched and kept safe.
- **Duty hours**: shift schedules, duty status changes and accomplishment
  reports of Tanods.
- **Earlier citizen reports** (the inbox was retired in October 2026; no new ones are added): reports sent in by residents, with the contact details
  they gave.
- **SMS records**: messages sent and received through the barangay's SMS
  gateway, and the list of residents who asked to receive barangay alerts.
- **Staff accounts**: names, usernames, roles, and official titles printed
  under signatures.

## Why we hold it

To respond to emergencies, to dispatch and account for Tanods on duty, to
keep the barangay's official incident records, and to send safety alerts to
residents who asked for them. The Local Government Code of 1991 makes the
Barangay Secretary the custodian of barangay records.

## Who can see what

| Role | Can see |
|---|---|
| Admin (Chief Tanod) | Dispatch, Tanod positions, incidents without narratives, users, devices, audit log, system settings |
| Secretary | Everything the Admin sees, plus confidential narratives and complainant/respondent details, and incident status changes |
| Punong Barangay | Dashboard, maps, reports, approvals; individual incident pages reachable from the dashboard; no list of all cases |
| Tanod (mobile app only) | Their own incidents, dispatches, shifts and reports |

Access is limited to the barangay's own records. No role can see another
barangay's records.

## How long we keep it

- **Confidential narratives**: deleted from the system one year after the
  incident is recorded, unless the record is under legal hold. The barangay
  keeps any official blotter in its own binders. **[to be confirmed by the
  barangay]**
- **Incident records**: kept 7 years, as the barangay's official record.
- **Tanod positions, duty status, shift schedules and notifications**: kept 1 year.
- **SMS message log and unconverted citizen reports**: kept 1 year.
- **Audit log**: kept 7 years.
- **Accomplishment reports, availability, school check-ins, referrals and
  Annex D term reports**: **[to be confirmed by the barangay]** — no deletion
  is done yet.
- **Backups**: **[to be confirmed by the barangay]**.

## Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may:

- **Ask to know** what personal data the barangay holds about you, and how it is used.
- **Ask for access** to a copy of your personal data.
- **Ask for correction** of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Object** to the processing of your data in certain cases.
- **Withdraw consent**, for example to receive SMS alerts. Ask to be removed
  from the list; your number is marked opted-out.
- **Complain** to the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights
  were violated.

Some records cannot be deleted on request while the law requires the
barangay to keep them (for example, incident records under the 7-year
retention, or a record under legal hold). The barangay will tell you if this
applies.

## For barangay staff using the dashboard

- Do not share your login. Log out when you leave the workstation.
- Do not print, export or send records unless your role requires it. Exports may
  contain personal data.
- Report any loss of a device or any suspected misuse to the Data Protection
  Officer and the Punong Barangay at once.
