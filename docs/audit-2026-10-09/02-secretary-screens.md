# Audit 02 - Secretary (Records and Reports Officer) web experience

Date: 2026-10-09. Method: static read of web/src, web/css and the controllers they call; `node web/scripts/verify-web-wiring.mjs` run (779 passed, 0 failed). Nothing was run in a browser, nothing was printed, no files other than this report were written. Paths are relative to the repo root; `pages/` means `web/src/pages/`.

Severity: P0 blocks safe use or creates legal exposure. P1 wrong or misleading. P2 friction. P3 polish. Effort: S under half a day, M 1-2 days, L more.

Summary: no P0. The confidentiality plumbing is genuinely sound (see "What is solid"). The problems are (a) places where the UI states things that are untrue or fabricated, (b) a silent 90-day narrative deletion the Secretary is never told about, (c) official printouts that can go out with blank or wrong content without warning, and (d) intake and records gaps that stop a Secretary doing a full day of records work.

## What is solid

- Confidentiality at the API: `raw_narrative`, `complainant_name`, `respondent_name` and `complainant_contact_number` are added only when the role is secretary (`backend/controllers/IncidentsController.php:625-633`), and every Secretary read is audited as `raw_narrative_viewed` with no content (`:637`). PATCH rejects `raw_narrative` (`:1198`).
- No narrative leakage found in list views, search or the printouts I read: the incident list and `GET /search` match only display id, type and numeric id (`IncidentsController.php:184-231`, `SearchController.php:23`); the Annex C-1 print uses the separate `c1_*` fields (`pages/school-zones.js:487-498`) with a "do not name students or victims" notice (`:414`, `components/IncidentCaseCards.js:~196`); the Referral Log shows only ids, type, destination, date, reference number (`pages/referral-log.js:11-14`); page titles are page names only (`web/src/main.js:200-210`); narrative and party fields are rendered with `textContent` or `escapeHtml`.
- Blotter/KP positioning is clean in user-facing wording: no screen offers a blotter, case register or Lupon packet. Only Admin Audit Log labels for legacy action names remain (`pages/audit-log.js:65-67`). The Referral Log is explicitly a delegation log; duplicate and related actions are links, never merges or deletions (`pages/incident-detail.js:756`, `IncidentCaseCards.js:479`). (S-04 "Under Investigation" is the one wording exception.)
- Four data states are present and generally good: approvals (each queue loads and fails on its own), referral log, accomplishment reports (list, detail, scans), school zones tabs, the case cards. Wiring check 779 passed, 0 failed.
- Segregation of duties is visible in the UI: preparer cannot note/approve (`pages/accomplishment-reports.js` buildActions, `pages/school-zones.js:869-872`); return requires a reason; "Mark as submitted" requires the Mayor-office receipt details and warns it cannot be undone.
- Print layouts follow the form shape: letterhead, title, pursuant line, instructions sheet after a page break, repeated thead and non-splitting rows (`web/css/pages/tanod-workflow.css:314-320`), a named landscape page for Annex B and C-1 (`:392`), a 31-row accomplishment log with shaded days past month end, and a received-by / copy-furnished block on Annex D.
- Report channel is captured at intake (`pages/incident-management.js:1243-1262`, `web/src/utils/reportChannel.js`) and shown on the record.
