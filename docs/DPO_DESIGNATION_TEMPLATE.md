## Republic of the Philippines
## Province of Sorsogon, Municipality of Pilar
## Barangay __________________________

### SANGGUNIANG BARANGAY RESOLUTION NO. __________ (Series of ________)
*(or: BARANGAY OFFICE ORDER NO. __________, if the Punong Barangay designates)*

**A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING A DATA PROTECTION OFFICER FOR THE BARANGAY OF
__________________ AND DEFINING HER/HIS DUTIES UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10173
(DATA PRIVACY ACT OF 2012)**

**WHEREAS**, the Barangay processes personal information of residents,
complainants, respondents, barangay personnel and volunteers in the course
of its public functions, including through the Baranguard dispatch and
records system;

**WHEREAS**, Republic Act No. 10173, its Implementing Rules and Regulations,
and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission require the Barangay,
as a personal information controller, to designate an individual
accountable for its compliance;

**WHEREAS**, the Barangay Secretary is the records custodian under Section
394(c) of Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991) — *(the
Council may choose any suitable officer; this line is a note, not a
requirement)*;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, be it resolved by the Sangguniang Barangay of
__________________ that:

**Section 1. Designation.** ______________________________ (name),
______________________ (position), is hereby designated as the Data
Protection Officer (DPO) of the Barangay, effective ____________________
(date).

**Section 2. Duties.** The DPO shall, among others:

1. Monitor the Barangay's compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012,
   its IRR, and NPC issuances. *(verify wording against the Act: Sec 21)*
2. Advise the Punong Barangay and the Sangguniang Barangay on personal
   data processing, including the use of the Baranguard system.
3. Be the contact point for data subjects who ask about their personal
   data, and for the National Privacy Commission. *(verify: Sec 21)*
4. Keep the Barangay's records of processing and privacy documents up to
   date (see the checklist in Section 4).
5. Lead the response to any personal data breach, including the
   notification steps in the Barangay's breach response runbook
   (`docs/BREACH_RESPONSE_RUNBOOK.md`). *(verify: Sec 20(f))*
6. Ensure that the Barangay's security measures for personal data are
   reasonable and appropriate. *(verify: Sec 20(a), (c))*
7. Recommend to the Punong Barangay any changes to the Barangay's
   personal data processing, including the scope of access to records.

**Section 3. Support.** The Punong Barangay shall give the DPO the time,
access to records and the Baranguard admin tools needed to perform these
duties. The DPO shall not be dismissed or penalised for performing them.
*(verify wording against the Act and the IRR)*

**Section 4. Privacy documents on file.** The Barangay shall keep the
following current:

- [ ] Personal data inventory (`docs/DATA_INVENTORY.md`, adapted for the Barangay)
- [ ] Privacy impact assessment (`docs/PRIVACY_IMPACT_ASSESSMENT.md`)
- [ ] Privacy notices for citizens, Tanods and SMS subscribers (`docs/PRIVACY_NOTICES.md`)
- [ ] Web privacy notice for staff (`docs/PRIVACY_NOTICE_WEB_DRAFT.md`, once adopted)
- [ ] Breach response runbook (`docs/BREACH_RESPONSE_RUNBOOK.md`) and breach log
- [ ] Records retention decision (narrative, accomplishment reports, check-ins, referrals, Annex D)
- [ ] Data sharing and outsourcing arrangement with the municipality or the system operator, if any
- [ ] NPC registration or sworn declaration status (verify circular thresholds)
- [ ] Data subject request log (access, correction, objection)

**Section 5. Effectivity.** This Resolution takes effect immediately upon
its approval and posting in the Barangay bulletin boards for three (3)
consecutive weeks. *(verify posting rule with the municipal office)*

## Duties summary (plain language, for the DPO's own reference)

*Taken from RA 10173 Sec 20 and Sec 21 as the audit read them in the
primary source. Labelled "verify wording against the Act": the wording
above is a plain-language paraphrase, not the statute's text.*

- **Keep the barangay compliant** with the Data Privacy Act. (verify: Sec 21)
- **Be the contact point** for the NPC and for people asking about their data. (verify: Sec 21)
- **Lead breach handling**, including telling the NPC and affected people. (verify: Sec 20(f))
- **Keep security reasonable**: access limited to need, safeguards in place. (verify: Sec 20(a), (c))
- **Advise the leaders** on new uses of personal data, such as new system features. (verify: Sec 21)
