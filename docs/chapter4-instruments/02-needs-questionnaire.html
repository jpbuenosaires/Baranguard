Introduction

Baranguard is a locally hosted, offline-first barangay intelligence and emergency dispatch system for the four pilot barangays of Pilar, Sorsogon (Dao, Binanuahan, Marifosque and Banuyo). It supports real-time tanod dispatch, GPS tracking, SOS alerts and offline field capture. It complements the DILG Barangay Information Management System (BIMSS) and does not replace it: the barangay case records stay in the barangay's own records and in BIMSS. This form is one of the data-gathering instruments of the capstone study at Bicol University (IT 124).

Purpose of this form. To learn how incident reporting, dispatch, record keeping, reporting and approvals are done now, so the problems the system should address can be identified. It is not a test.
Scope and respondents. Barangay personnel only: Chief Tanod, Barangay Secretary, Punong Barangay, Kagawad and tanods. Residents are not respondents.

Purpose

This questionnaire gathers the views of barangay personnel on how incident reporting, dispatch of tanods, record keeping, reporting and approvals are currently done in the barangay. The answers will help the proponents identify the problems that the Baranguard system should address. It is not a test and there are no right or wrong answers.

How to answer

For each statement, tick one box that best matches how much you agree. Every statement describes the current process as working well. Therefore, agree means the current process works well, and a low rating (Disagree or Strongly Disagree) means the current process has a problem. Please answer based on your own experience in the last twelve (12) months.

Glossary of Terms

TermMeaning in this study
dispatchThe assignment of an on-duty tanod to an incident by the Chief Tanod (Admin).
dispatch offerA request sent to on-duty tanods at night (18:00 to 06:00, Manila time); the first tanod to accept receives the dispatch.
roster / published shiftA tanod duty schedule. A shift is a draft until an official with approval authority publishes it; a tanod sees published shifts only.
availabilityThe times a tanod states he or she can serve; the roster is built from it.
accomplishment reportThe monthly report of a tanod's work, prepared by the tanod, then noted and approved by officials. It serves as the tanod's attendance record.
referralA record that an incident was handed over to another agency (for example PNP, BFP or EMS). It records the handover, not the agency's acceptance.
Safer School Zones (Annex B, C-1, D)School-zone reporting: Annex B is the school list, C-1 the per-incident summary, and D the term report.
SOSAn emergency alert raised by a tanod from the phone. If the server cannot be reached, the phone sends an SMS to one configured backup contact.
demo setupA copy of the system loaded with fictitious data, used for testing and evaluation. No real record is used.
