Introduction

Baranguard is a locally hosted, offline-first barangay intelligence and emergency dispatch system for the four pilot barangays of Pilar, Sorsogon (Dao, Binanuahan, Marifosque and Banuyo). It supports real-time tanod dispatch, GPS tracking, SOS alerts and offline field capture. It complements the DILG Barangay Information Management System (BIMSS) and does not replace it: the barangay case records stay in the barangay's own records and in BIMSS. This form is one of the data-gathering instruments of the capstone study at Bicol University (IT 124).

Purpose of this form. To rate the quality of the Baranguard system against the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality model, and to gather comments for improvement.
Scope and respondents. Evaluators outside the development team: end users (tanods, Secretary, Chief Tanod), client representatives (Punong Barangay, Kagawad) and IT practitioners or faculty. The evaluation uses the demo setup with fictitious data only.

Instructions

  1. This questionnaire evaluates the Baranguard system against the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality model. It is answered by evaluators who are not members of the development team.
  2. The evaluation is done on the demonstration setup, which contains fictitious data only. No real incident, citizen or tanod record is used or shown.
  3. Use the system first (the facilitator will guide a short set of tasks), then rate each indicator. Mark one box per row: 5, 4, 3, 2 or 1. Mark N/A only if the feature was not shown to you or you did not use it.
  4. Answer the parts assigned to your evaluator group (see the tag on each section). Group D evaluators answer every part. Groups A, B and C answer Part 3 and the optional Part 4.
  5. Rate the interface your role actually uses. Tanod (Group A) use the mobile app only. The Secretary, Punong Barangay and Kagawad (Groups B and C) use the web dashboard only; a Kagawad signs in with a Secretary account that carries approval authority. The Chief Tanod (Admin, Group B) uses the web dashboard and the small Chief Tanod console in the mobile app (SOS acknowledge, dispatch assign and cancel, night dispatch offers, read-only incidents). Group D uses both. Mark N/A for any indicator about a function that is not part of your interface.
  6. There are no right or wrong answers. Please rate what was observed, and use the comment lines to explain low ratings.

Part 6. Consolidated Tally Sheet (proponents only)

The proponents copy the marks from each returned form into one tally. For each indicator, count how many evaluators marked 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 (N/A answers are not counted). Only the groups assigned to a characteristic are counted for it.

Formula

Weighted mean (WM) = (5·f5 + 4·f4 + 3·f3 + 2·f2 + 1·f1) ÷ N

where fk is the number of evaluators who gave the score k and N = f5+f4+f3+f2+f1 is the number of valid answers for that indicator. The characteristic mean is the mean of the indicator means of that characteristic. Interpret each mean with the rating scale in the Instructions.

Worked example (FICTITIOUS numbers, for illustration only; not a result of this study).

Suppose a made-up indicator received N = 10 answers: f5 = 4, f4 = 3, f3 = 2, f2 = 1, f1 = 0.
Σ = 5(4) + 4(3) + 3(2) + 2(1) + 1(0) = 20 + 12 + 6 + 2 + 0 = 40.
WM = 40 ÷ 10 = 4.00, which falls in 3.41 – 4.20 and is interpreted as Very Satisfactory.
If a made-up characteristic had indicator means 4.00, 3.60 and 4.40, its characteristic mean would be (4.00 + 3.60 + 4.40) ÷ 3 = 4.00.

Glossary of Terms

TermMeaning in this study
dispatchThe assignment of an on-duty tanod to an incident by the Chief Tanod (Admin).
dispatch offerA request sent to on-duty tanods at night (18:00 to 06:00, Manila time); the first tanod to accept receives the dispatch.
roster / published shiftA tanod duty schedule. A shift is a draft until an official with approval authority publishes it; a tanod sees published shifts only.
availabilityThe times a tanod states he or she can serve; the roster is built from it.
accomplishment reportThe monthly report of a tanod's work, prepared by the tanod, then noted and approved by officials. It serves as the tanod's attendance record.
referralA record that an incident was handed over to another agency (for example PNP, BFP or EMS). It records the handover, not the agency's acceptance.
Safer School Zones (Annex B, C-1, D)School-zone reporting: Annex B is the school list, C-1 the per-incident summary, and D the term report.
SOSAn emergency alert raised by a tanod from the phone. If the server cannot be reached, the phone sends an SMS to one configured backup contact.
demo setupA copy of the system loaded with fictitious data, used for testing and evaluation. No real record is used.
