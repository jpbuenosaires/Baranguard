Privacy notice

This dashboard is used by barangay staff to dispatch Tanods, record incidents and keep barangay records. It holds personal information, so access is limited by role and records are kept for set periods.

What this dashboard holds

  • Incident reports: the type, place, time and status of an incident, and the names and contact numbers of complainants and respondents where recorded.
  • Confidential narratives: the written account of an incident. Only the Barangay Secretary can read it.
  • Tanod positions: the location of Tanods on duty, while they are on duty, so they can be dispatched and kept safe.
  • Duty hours: shift schedules, duty status changes and accomplishment reports of Tanods.
  • Earlier citizen reports: reports sent in by residents before the citizen inbox was retired (October 2026), with the contact details they gave. Nothing new is added; old ones are deleted on schedule.
  • SMS records: messages sent and received through the barangay's SMS gateway, and the list of residents who asked to receive barangay alerts.
  • Staff accounts: names, usernames, roles, and official titles printed under signatures.

Why we hold it

To respond to emergencies, to dispatch and account for Tanods on duty, to keep the barangay's official incident records, and to send safety alerts to residents who asked for them. The Local Government Code of 1991 makes the Barangay Secretary the custodian of barangay records.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may exercise the following rights:

Right to Be Informed

Ask to know what personal data the barangay holds about you, and how it is used.

Right of Access

Ask for access to a formal copy of your personal records and processing log.

Right to Rectification

Ask for immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to Object

Object to the processing of your data in specific discretionary barangay cases.

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent to receive SMS emergency alerts; your number is immediately marked opted-out.

File a Complaint

Complain directly to the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if your privacy rights were infringed.

Some records cannot be deleted on request while the law requires the barangay to keep them (for example, incident records under the 7-year statutory retention, or a record under legal hold). The barangay will notify you in writing if an exception applies.

This list of rights is to be checked against RA 10173 Section 16 before the notice is adopted.

For barangay staff using the dashboard

  • Credential Security: Do not share your login credentials. Log out whenever you leave the workstation unattended.
  • Authorized Data Export: Do not print, export or transmit records unless your specific role requires it. Exported files contain sensitive citizen information.
  • Immediate Incident Reporting: Report any loss of an authorized mobile device or any suspected misuse to the Data Protection Officer and Punong Barangay immediately.
